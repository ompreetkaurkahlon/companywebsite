ABOUT M90

Built on engineering, run on technology.

Mundhe90 Technologies Pvt Ltd — a.k.a. M90 Technologies. An integrated multidisciplinary enterprise uniting software intelligence and civil physical infrastructure.

OUR STORY

From 2019 to today

Founded in 2019, M90 Technologies began with a simple premise: that growing cities need partners who understand both software and steel. Over the years, we've built the GIS and property-tax systems that make local governance more transparent, alongside the energy and water infrastructure that keeps communities running.

What connects our work across such different domains is the same discipline in each: careful engineering, delivered reliably. Today, we stand as a trusted partner for municipal corporations, smart cities, and private enterprises across Maharashtra and beyond.

VISION

Where we're headed

To be the infrastructure and technology partner that cities and institutions turn to first — trusted equally for the software systems that modernize governance and the civil, energy, and water infrastructure that public life depends on.

Smart Governance Sustainable Utilities Resilient Cities
MISSION

How we work

We bring engineering and technology together under one roof. From GIS-based property tax systems and e-governance tools that make local administration more transparent, to the roads, water networks, and power infrastructure that growing communities need — we deliver work that holds up.

OUR VALUES
